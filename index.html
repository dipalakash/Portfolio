About Me

Welcome to my corner of the internet! I'm a Full Stack Developer who loves bringing ideas to life through code. My journey into web development began during my college years when I built my first website – a simple portfolio that sparked a passion that has only grown stronger.

Today, I specialize in the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, and Node.js), creating full-scale web applications that are not just functional, but also intuitive and user-friendly. I've had the privilege of working on diverse projects ranging from e-commerce platforms to real-time collaboration tools, each teaching me something new and valuable.

What drives me is the perfect blend of creativity and logic that web development offers. I love the challenge of solving complex problems, optimizing performance, and creating seamless user experiences.
